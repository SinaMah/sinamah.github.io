01

From research question to reliable software.

I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Engineering, Software, at Iran University of Science and Technology. My current academic work is centered on distributed systems, cloud computing, multi-cloud computing, and AI systems, with an interest in understanding how complex computational systems can be designed and operated reliably.

I currently work with the Distributed Systems Lab (DS Lab) under the supervision of Mohsen Sharifi. My background includes software development and systems-oriented coursework, together with hands-on work using C++, C#, Python, SQL, Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, cloud platforms, Apache Spark, and GitHub.

My goal is to connect research questions with practical implementations and to develop work that is technically clear, experimentally grounded, and useful beyond a single prototype.
